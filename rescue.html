01 · EMERGENCY RESPONSE

Every second can matter.

Rescue begins with understanding the situation. Our network works quickly while keeping the animal's safety and wellbeing at the centre.

FIELD OPERATIONS

A calm response in a critical moment.

From the first report to safe transport, our coordinators connect the right people and resources to each case.

01

Report

A case is received and the immediate situation is assessed.

02

Assess

Responders identify urgency, risks and the safest approach.

03

Rescue

The animal is safely moved away from immediate danger.

04

Stabilise

Medical care, food, rest and temporary shelter follow.

02 · OUR METHOD

Rescue is a process of trust.

A successful rescue does not end when an animal leaves the street. Our work continues through every stage of recovery.

01

Locate

We understand where the animal is, what danger they face and what support is required.

02

Recover

Safe handling, transport and immediate care help the animal move beyond crisis.

03

Rehabilitate

Medical attention, nutrition and patient rehabilitation rebuild strength and trust.

04

Rehome

Responsible placement connects each animal with people prepared for their long-term needs.
